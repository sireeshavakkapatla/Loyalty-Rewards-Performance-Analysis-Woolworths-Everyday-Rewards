## Loyalty Rewards Performance Analysis – Woolworths Everyday Rewards

### Quantitative Outcomes

1,000 customers registered across Melbourne stores, generating 5,508 transactions and 874K loyalty points earned.
Customers redeemed 42K points, representing a 5.10% redemption rate, slightly above the corporate threshold of 4.79%.
Sydney store contributed 8.67% of total points earned and 9.23% of points redeemed, showing higher-than-average customer engagement.

### Identified top redeemers:

Bruce Coffey redeemed the highest (581 points)
Stephen Jordan earned the highest (1,086 points)
Quantified unused value with ~71,970 unredeemed points remaining in Sydney, indicating potential opportunity for upsell or activation campaigns.
Membership breakdown showed 89.3% active customers, and 5.5% suspended, supporting healthy loyalty program participation.

### Redemption behavior:

Shopping discount redemptions: 67.5%
Fuel discount redemptions: ~21%
Qantas Points: 6.08%
Highlights the dominance of supermarket-driven reward consumption.

Store-level contribution:

Best performers (earned): Parramatta 88K, Bondi 85K, Campbelltown 84K
Best performers (redeemed): Hornsby 82K, Bankstown 82K
Scatter plot analysis revealed positive correlation between high earners and high redeemers.

## Qualitative Insights & Business Impact

### Healthy program engagement ## : 
Consistently strong point earning patterns across 2023–2024 indicate effective customer retention and ongoing shopping frequency.
### Low redemption rate (<6%) 
signals an opportunity to drive targeted campaigns encouraging customers to redeem more frequently, lifting perceived program value.
### Large buffer of unredeemed points 
suggests both liability for the business and an opportunity for re-engagement through “use your points” nudges.
### Customer behavior segmentation revealed:
High-value earners often overlap with medium-tier redeemers → potential for targeted incentives.
Several customers earn heavily but redeem minimally → opportunity to boost redemption engagement.
### Store-level analysis showed different behavioral patterns:
Stores like Campbelltown and Hornsby showed strong redemption behavior → replicate engagement strategy across underperforming regions.
Stores with high earning but low redemption indicate customers value accumulating points for future benefits.
### Membership health: 
High active status (89.3%) confirms strong customer stickiness and loyalty program relevance.
Time trend analysis uncovered seasonal earning spikes (June–August), suggesting campaign or event-driven influences worth replicating.
